# Sources are kept in their official language, not translated

German law exists only in German, and EU law exists in both English and German. The Corpus holds every Source in its official language, and retrieval works across languages, so a German § can answer an English question. Translating during ingestion was rejected: it would create unofficial translations of law that must be re-translated on every amendment, which multiplies the freshness problem. It would also make citations differ from the authoritative wording. Translation happens only at answer time, next to the original quote.
