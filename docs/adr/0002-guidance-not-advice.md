# Guidance, never personalised legal advice

The guide gives practical, non-personalised Guidance ("this is what the law requires; these are the steps founders typically take"). It stops at defined Escalation Triggers and refers the Founder to a lawyer. Pure legal *information* with disclaimers was rejected as too unhelpful for founders. Personalised advice was rejected because in Germany it is a legal service reserved for lawyers (§ 2 RDG), and in the US it risks unauthorised practice of law. The Founder Profile tailors *which* law is relevant; it never turns an answer into advice about the Founder's specific case.

## Consequences

The statement "**This is general legal information, not legal advice. It does not replace consultation with a qualified lawyer.**" appears on every surface:
- the README (at the top)
- the first-run screen, which must be acknowledged before the first question
- a persistent banner in the UI
- the footer of every answer
- every export (JSON, Markdown, PDF)
- every API response
- every MCP tool result

A disclaimer alone does not make personalised advice permissible. The behaviour (Guidance plus Escalation Triggers) carries the weight; the disclaimer makes it explicit.
